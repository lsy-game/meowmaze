Privacy Policy

Updated November 9, 2026

1. The game

The game can be played without creating an account. Game progress, unlocked levels, virtual fish balance, home selections, tutorial progress and sound preferences are saved locally on your device. The game does not include an active advertising SDK, an analytics SDK, online account synchronization or real-money purchase processing.

We do not operate a server that receives your game progress in this version. Local progress is used to remember your settings and continue the game. Device or operating-system backup services may back up application data according to your own device settings and their separate terms.

2. This website

This site presents game information and game images. It has no registration form, email collection form, advertising integration or application analytics added by the game team. Images, scripts and styles are served from the same site.

The hosting provider must process connection information, such as IP address, browser information, requested pages and request times, to deliver the website and maintain its security. Its infrastructure may retain operational logs. We have not independently verified the hosting provider's log-retention periods; they are not specified here.

3. Sharing and external services

The game has no configured advertising or analytics data-sharing integration. The website's hosting provider processes website requests to serve the site. If you follow an external link or use an operating-system service, that service's own privacy practices apply.

4. Keeping and deleting local progress

Local game data remains on your device until it is reset, removed or replaced. Use the game's reset controls to clear the progress those controls describe, or use Android's application storage controls to clear all app data. Uninstalling or clearing storage can permanently remove progress. Your device backup settings may affect whether a backup copy remains or is restored.

5. Children and personal information

The game has no account, public chat, user-upload feature or form requesting personal information. A parent or guardian may contact us about a child's personal information using the address below.

6. Security

This website uses HTTPS. The game stores game data locally and does not offer a cloud backup operated by the game team. No storage or network system can guarantee absolute security; keep access to your device protected.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when our services or data practices change. The updated date will appear at the top of this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact meowmaze@outlook.com. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, and use it to handle your request.
