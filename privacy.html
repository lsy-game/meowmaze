Privacy Policy

Preview draft · Updated October 30, 2026

Before the public game release

This draft describes the current preview build of MeowMaze and this promotional website. The operator identity and a working privacy contact have not yet been finalized. These details must be added, and this policy reviewed against the release build, before the game is submitted to Google Play. This is not a final release policy.

1. The current game preview

The current preview can be played without creating an account. Game progress, unlocked levels, virtual fish balance, home selections, tutorial progress and sound preferences are saved locally on your device. The preview does not include an active advertising SDK, an analytics SDK, online account synchronization or real-money purchase processing.

We do not operate a server that receives your game progress in this preview. Local progress is used to remember your settings and continue the game. Device or operating-system backup services may back up application data according to your own device settings and their separate terms.

2. This website

This site presents game information and preview images. It has no registration form, email collection form, advertising integration or application analytics added by the game team. Images, scripts and styles are served from the same site.

The hosting provider must process connection information, such as IP address, browser information, requested pages and request times, to deliver the website and maintain its security. Its infrastructure may retain operational logs. We have not independently verified the hosting provider's log-retention periods; they are not specified here.

3. Sharing and external services

The current game preview has no configured advertising or analytics data-sharing integration. The website's hosting provider processes website requests to serve the site. If you follow an external link or use an operating-system service, that service's own privacy practices apply.

4. Keeping and deleting local progress

Local game data remains on your device until it is reset, removed or replaced. Use the game's reset controls to clear the progress those controls describe, or use Android's application storage controls to clear all app data. Uninstalling or clearing storage can permanently remove progress. Your device backup settings may affect whether a backup copy remains or is restored.

5. Children and personal information

The preview has no account, public chat, user-upload feature or form requesting personal information. The final intended audience and applicable child-privacy requirements must be confirmed before release. Please do not send personal information through any future support channel unless it is needed to resolve your request.

6. Security

This website uses HTTPS. The preview stores game data locally and does not offer a cloud backup operated by the game team. No storage or network system can guarantee absolute security; keep access to your device protected.

7. Changes

If advertising, analytics, accounts, purchases or other online services are added, this policy will need to be updated to identify the services, data practices, retention periods and relevant choices before those features launch. The update date will appear at the top of this page.

8. Operator and privacy contact

Pending before release: the game operator's identity and a working contact method for privacy requests. No contact address is published yet. Please check this page again before the game's public release.
